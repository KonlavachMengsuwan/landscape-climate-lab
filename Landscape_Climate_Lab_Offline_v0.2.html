What the trees represent

Crowns are solid geometric shapes. Moving and resizing them changes their shadows. Crown gaps are not modeled. The temperature layer adds assumed surface heat exchange without local air-cooling feedback.

What this layer means

Daytime ground and water surfaces under current conditions, sampled at 16 points per tile. Buildings and boardwalks are excluded. No heat storage, canopy temperature, or air-cooling feedback is modeled.
